# 11 — Demo & Devpost Strategy

## Demo story (5 min, live)

1. **Open mission control** (10s) — Pulse 0–1000, map, alert list. "Dashboards show
   disasters after they happen."
2. **Emerging anomaly** (20s) — rainfall anomaly + soil moisture spike near Adyar;
   an amber risk appears with a confidence meter. "EarthPulse predicted this
   36 hours ahead."
3. **Causal chain** (30s) — rain → drainage headroom → flood risk; nodes explain
   *why*, SHAP bars show what matters. "Not just a number — a mechanism."
4. **Uncertainty honesty** (15s) — low-confidence zone triggers the AI debate;
   agents argue over conflicting evidence. "It knows what it doesn't know."
5. **What-if sandbox** (40s) — pre-position pumps / reservoir release sliders →
   before/after damage curve, carbon ledger. "Simulate how to stop it."
6. **Operational checklist** (20s) — stakeholder-specific actions with citations.
7. **Crisis mode** (15s) — severity threshold crossed live via WebSocket; red banner,
   alert ticker. "Alerts people can act on."
